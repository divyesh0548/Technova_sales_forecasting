Development roadmap
Sales forecasting for plates, using only the columns in Useful-columns.txt.

What we will forecast
- Monthly quantity (qtynos) for the next 12 months.
- Monthly sales value (NetAmt) estimated from forecasted quantity and recent average price.
- Series grain:
  Company x Customer x Product Family x Manufacturer or Trading x Month
- Territory and Customer Type stay as attributes of each series, not extra forecast keys.

What we will not do in this version
- Do not use the Product column. Every row is "PLATES", so it does not split demand.
- Do not forecast at item / product-code level. That column is not in the useful list and would create too many sparse series.
- Do not train two model families at once. Start with one global model.
- Do not build a dashboard yet. Deliver forecasts as Parquet plus a small Excel summary.
- Do not use statistical reconciliation methods. Roll totals up by summing the detailed forecasts.

Columns we will keep
1. Company                  TISL, TISL - DUBAI
2. Territory                country or state of the customer
3. Customer                 customer name
4. Customer Type            type of customer
5. InvDate                  invoice date, converted to month
6. NetAmt                   sales value
7. qtynos                   quantity
8. Product Family           VIO, ANG, CTC, TSL, TDL, DOP, ICTP, DIG, MET (and missing)
9. Manufacturer or Trading  MFG, TRD


Step 1 — Clean the useful columns

Read Merged_sales_data.parquet and keep only the nine columns above.

- Strip spaces and standardize text in Company, Territory, Customer, Customer Type, Product Family, and Manufacturer or Trading.
- Convert InvDate to a real date, then to a month (first day of month).
- Convert qtynos and NetAmt to numbers. Rows that cannot be converted are dropped.
- Keep negative quantity or value. They are returns and should reduce the monthly total.
- Missing Product Family: count it first. If it is rare, drop those rows. If it is common, label it "Unknown" and keep it.
- Save the cleaned invoice-level data as Cleaned_sales_data.parquet.


Step 2 — Build the monthly series

Aggregate to:
Company x Customer x Product Family x Manufacturer or Trading x Month

For each group calculate:
- qty            sum of qtynos
- net_amt        sum of NetAmt
- invoice_count  number of invoice rows
- avg_price      net_amt / qty when qty is not zero

Attach Territory and Customer Type as the most frequent value inside that group.
If a customer is tied to more than one territory or type, keep the most frequent one and note the conflict during EDA.

Fill missing months inside each series with qty = 0 and net_amt = 0, from the first month that series appears through the latest month in the data. This matters because many customers do not buy every month.

Save as Monthly_sales.parquet.


Step 3 — Exploratory analysis

Answer these questions before modeling. Use group totals, pivots, and time plots. Correlation alone is not enough.

- Company: how TISL and TISL - DUBAI differ in quantity and value.
- Territory: which territories contribute the most, and whether they are growing.
- Customer Type: which types produce more quantity and value.
- Product Family x Territory: which families sell where.
- Customer x Product Family: which families each large customer buys, and how often.
- Manufacturer or Trading: MFG vs TRD quantity, value, and trend.
- Quantity vs NetAmt: whether value moves with quantity or is driven by price.
- Month: seasonal pattern and which months are strong or weak.
- Intermittent demand: share of series with many zero months.
- Concentration: share of quantity held by the top customers.

Save a short EDA note in the notebook. Change the grain only if EDA shows Manufacturer or Trading does not split demand.


Step 4 — Features

Build features only from months before the month being predicted.

From history, for both qty and net_amt:
- lag_1, lag_2, lag_3, lag_6, lag_12
- rolling mean of the previous 3, 6, and 12 months
- active_months_12: how many of the last 12 months had qty > 0
- months_since_last_sale

From the calendar:
- month number
- quarter

From the series attributes (known before the month):
- Company
- Territory
- Customer Type
- Product Family
- Manufacturer or Trading

Do not use same-month qty, NetAmt, invoice_count, or avg_price as inputs. Those are the result we are predicting.


Step 5 — Train and compare

Primary target: monthly qty.
Value: forecast qty multiplied by that series' average price over the previous 3 non-zero months. Add a separate NetAmt model only if that price estimate is clearly poor.

Models:
1. Seasonal naive: same month last year, or last month if last year is missing.
2. LightGBM global model on all series together.

Split by time, not at random. Hold out the last 6 months as the first test. If that looks stable, check one earlier 6-month window.

Compare with MAE and WAPE (weighted absolute percentage error) on quantity. Also report error after summing to Customer, Product Family, Territory, and Company.

Keep LightGBM if it beats seasonal naive on the holdout. Try CatBoost only if LightGBM does not.


Step 6 — Produce the 12-month forecast

- Forecast the next 12 months at the series grain.
- Build each future month using lags from history plus previously forecasted months.
- Aggregate by summing to Customer, Product Family, Territory, and Company.
- Also give cumulative quantity and value for 1, 3, 6, and 12 months.
- Save:
  Forecast_monthly.parquet   one row per series per future month
  Forecast_summary.xlsx      the four aggregate views

Uncertainty ranges and a dashboard come after this forecast is trusted.
