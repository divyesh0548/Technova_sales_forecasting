1. Customer-wise sales forecasting

Predict the value and quantity each customer will purchase over the next 1, 3, 6, or 12 months.

Example: Expected monthly sales for customer code 231240, including growth or decline against the previous year.


2. Product-wise sales forecasting

Predict future sales quantities and amounts for each individual SKU (sdlitm), along with demand for a product family.

Example: Forecast future demand for IB45789 and the entire VioStar product family.


3. Customer × Product forecasting

Predict which products a particular customer is likely to purchase, in what quantity, and when.

Example: Customer 231240 may purchase 500 units of Product A and 800 units of Product B next month.
